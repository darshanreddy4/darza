# Business documentation

This folder explains, in plain language, **what the store does, who can do what, and how every business situation is handled** from the first visit to a refund. It is written for owners, admins, staff and anyone who needs to understand or demonstrate the service. No programming knowledge is needed.

> Prefer pictures? Open **[infographic.html](infographic.html)** in any browser. It is a single page with the whole service as visual diagrams (works offline, prints well, adapts to phones).

## Words used in these documents

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Own product** | A product you sell and ship yourself. Has stock, a cart, checkout and an invoice |
| **Affiliate product** | A product sold by a partner marketplace. The shop shows it and sends the visitor to the partner, tracking the click |
| **Guest** | Someone browsing without an account |
| **Customer** | A signed-in shopper. **Verified** means they confirmed an email address or a mobile number |
| **Staff** | A team member with the smaller console for day-to-day orders, returns and questions |
| **Admin** | An owner-level user with full control, including money movements and settings |
| **COD** | Cash on delivery: the customer pays when the parcel arrives |
| **Online payment** | Paid in advance by card, UPI or net banking through Razorpay |
| **Paise** | One hundredth of a rupee. The system stores money in paise so there are no rounding errors; screens show rupees |
| **Payout** | For cash-on-delivery refunds: the manual transfer of money to the customer's UPI ID, recorded in the system |

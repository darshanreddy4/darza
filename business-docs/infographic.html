The order tracker

Every order moves through the same statuses. The customer sees the badge and is told about each change.

Awaiting payment→ Paid→ Shipped→ Delivered

Online payment path. Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically after 30 minutes and the stock goes back.

Order placed→ Shipped→ Delivered

Cash on delivery path. The customer pays the courier.

Cancelled before shipping, by the customer or an admin. Stock returns; paid online orders are refunded.
Refunded after delivery, once every item has been returned and refunded.

Who moves it

Staff and admins move orders to Paid, Shipped and Delivered. Only admins cancel or refund an order from the console.

Delivered unlocks

The invoice and the return window (7 days by default, set by the admin; 0 turns returns off).

Order details

Items, price breakdown, discount, delivery, payment method, cancellation reason, refund amount and WhatsApp choice.

How money moves

The two payment methods have different refund paths. Both are tracked so nothing is forgotten or paid twice.

Online payment automatic

  1. Customer pays by card, UPI or net banking in the secure Razorpay window.
  2. The payment is confirmed and the order becomes Paid.
  3. Cancel before shipping: refunded automatically to the same card or account.
  4. Return after delivery: when an admin presses Refund, the money goes back automatically (banks take 5 to 7 business days).
  5. A payment that arrives after the 30-minute window is refunded automatically.

Cash on delivery tracked by hand

  1. Customer pays the courier on delivery. Nothing to refund if cancelled before shipping.
  2. Return: the customer gives a UPI ID when asking.
  3. Admin presses Refund; the request appears under Payouts due with amount and UPI ID.
  4. Admin sends the money, then records the UPI reference with Mark as paid (once only).
  5. The customer is told, and sees "Paid to ... on ..." on their order.

Refund amount, worked example

Order: items of ₹800 and ₹200, coupon ₹100, delivery ₹50. Customer paid ₹950.

Return the ₹200 item

Refund = 200 − coupon share (100 × 200 / 1,000 = 20) = ₹180. Delivery is kept.

Return the ₹800 item later

Refund = everything still owed = 950 − 180 = ₹770, including delivery. Order becomes Refunded.

Cancel, return or refund: which one?

A customer chooses based on where the order is. The system enforces it.

Before shipping

Cancel order in My orders.

  • Pick a reason (and add a note)
  • Stock goes back at once
  • Paid online: refund starts automatically
  • If the refund cannot start, the cancel is undone

Shipped, not yet delivered

No cancel button.

  • The parcel is on its way
  • An admin can still cancel in the console
  • The customer can return it once delivered

After delivery, inside the window

Return or refund in My orders.

  • Pick items and quantities (part or all)
  • Staff approve or decline with a reason
  • Admin refunds when the item is back
  • Item goes back into stock

After the window

Return button is gone.

  • The deadline is shown on the order
  • The window length is an admin setting

Who can do what

Permissions are enforced by the server. Hiding a menu is not the only protection: even a guessed address is refused.

Guest

Anyone browsing, no account

  • Browse, search, compare
  • Use the cart, preview coupon and delivery fee
  • Follow partner links
  • Sign up or log in

Customer

Signed in; verified once email or phone is confirmed

  • Everything a guest can
  • Wishlist, stock alerts, addresses
  • Orders, invoices, cancel before shipping
  • Verified only: order, return, review, ask
  • Add sign-in methods, set password
  • Download or delete own data

Staff

Day-to-day team

  • See all orders; mark Paid, Shipped, Delivered
  • Approve or decline returns
  • Answer or delete questions
  • Look up products without costs
  • See the Attention list

Admin

Owner or senior manager

  • Everything staff can
  • Cancel and refund orders and returns; mark payouts paid
  • Products, categories, coupons, banners, home page
  • Settings, brand, users and roles
  • Analytics, reports, audit log

The built-in safety net

Server-enforced roles

Staff cannot move money or see costs, even by guessing addresses.

Audit log

Every admin and staff change is recorded, including blocked attempts.

Login protection

Five wrong passwords lock sign-in for 15 minutes. Codes are limited and stored hashed.

No overselling

Stock is reserved when the order is created; 40 buyers racing for 5 units were tested.

Secure sessions

Private cookies, origin checks, and a password change signs out every device.

Safe uploads and exports

Files are checked by content; spreadsheets cannot run formulas.

Your data, your choice

Customers can download or delete their data; invoices are kept as the law requires.

Monitoring and backups

Health checks, JSON logs, error alerts, and backup and restore scripts.

Runs anywhere

No lock-in. Move any piece by changing a setting, not the code.
